ShootDesk on iPhone Duo

A working replica of the app in HTML. Every tab, card and button here does what the real one does, on made-up shoots, agents and money. Nothing is real and nothing is sent anywhere.
Closed is the outer screen, one column. Open is the inner screen: the same pages with more room, two columns where it helps. Half-open is the laptop pose: open a shoot and the photo sits above the fold, the details below. Try: tap the big photo on Today, press and hold a photo on the shoot page, or Ask a suggested question.
